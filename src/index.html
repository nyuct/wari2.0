Journeys usually start with a destination in mind. But what about those that don’t seek a destination but an eternity. Answering the call of the Lord is a divine pull. And the journeys they invoke are other worldly. They defy the logic of distance and time. They shatter the challenges of dust and doubts. While the faithful walk on. Drenched in faith. Listening to an inner voice. Walking the rough roads. Braving temperamental weather. And wading through the miles.
These journeys are special. They are guided by an invisible hand and visible faith. The mesmerising draw of divinity and spiritual communions keep the tireless travellers going.

Seeking a blissful eternity

This is one such journey.
One that the world calls Wāri.

Devotees walk in disciplined, organized groups called dindis, chanting abhangs (devotional songs) to meet their beloved God, Vitthal. Led by the Palkhis (palanquins) of two of Maharashtra’s greatest saints: Sant Tukaram Maharaj from Dehu and Sant Dnyaneshwar Maharaj from Alandi. These palanquins carry the padukas (sacred sandals) of the saints, symbolizing their presence and blessings.
